Common questions

Do I need to be pregnant?

No. After is designed for women exploring another pregnancy after a C-section, including before conception.

Can After determine whether I'm eligible for VBAC?

No. A qualified maternity clinician must assess individual risks, benefits and options. We help you access the records and consultations needed for that discussion.

Does After replace my doctor or doula?

No. After provides nonclinical research and administrative coordination, not medical advice or clinical birth planning.
